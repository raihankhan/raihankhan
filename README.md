👋 About Me

I'm a Senior Platform Engineer at [@MakroPro](https://www.linkedin.com/company/makropro), building cloud-native platforms, internal developer platforms (IDPs), Custom Kubernetes Operators and agent-first automation that helps engineering teams ship faster and operate reliably at scale.

I work at the intersection of Platform Engineering, Distributed Systems, Cloud Infrastructure, and AI-driven Engineering. My focus is transforming complex infrastructure and operational challenges into scalable, self-service platforms and intelligent automation.

🎯 My next frontier: Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE)

I want to bridge deep systems engineering with real-world customer problems, working close to users, integrating complex systems, and delivering production-ready solutions with measurable impact.
